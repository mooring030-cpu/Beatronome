Beatronome Basic sample mapping

User-provided recordings were grouped by the numbered subdivision folders and their three recordings were ordered by filename/time as:
1) strong accent
2) weak/subdivision
3) normal main beat

Banks:
1 = Quarter
2 = 8th
3 = Triplet (all)
4 = Triplet (1 and 3)
5 = 16th (all)
6 = 16th (1 and 4)

Runtime role mapping:
- accented main beat -> strong
- normal main beat -> normal
- audible subdivision -> weak
